About Freebies Near Me

A map and directory of free giveaways, samples, and pop up events happening in every city we cover, right now.

What this is

Freebies Near Me is a running map and directory of free things happening in Toronto and New York: product giveaways, sample days, pop up activations, free classes and workshops, concerts, and more. Pick a city, then browse by map, by category, by neighbourhood, or by date on the events calendar, and see what's live right now versus what's coming up.

How listings get verified

Every listing starts as a submission, from a brand, an organizer, or someone in the community who spotted something worth sharing. Before anything goes live, submissions are automatically screened to filter out spam and inappropriate content, then reviewed by our team to confirm it's a real, currently available free listing with a real time and location. Nothing is published without that review.

Who it's for

Anyone looking for free things to doStudents, families, and anyone in our cities who wants to find what's free and nearby without digging through social media.
Brands and organizersA place to list a giveaway, sample day, or pop up in front of people already looking for exactly that. See the advertising options for Toronto or New York if you want extra visibility.
