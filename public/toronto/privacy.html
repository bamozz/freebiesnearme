Privacy Policy

Last updated August 17, 2026

What we collect

Freebies Near Me collects information you give us directly: an email address if you sign up for giveaway alerts, and the details you provide when submitting a listing, an advertise inquiry, or feedback (brand name, event details, contact email, and an optional photo). We also use standard site analytics (Google Analytics and Vercel Analytics) to understand how the site is used, which collects general usage data like pages visited and browser type, not tied to your name.

How we use your email

Emails collected through the subscriber signup and submission forms are used only for giveaway alerts and listing moderation, for example to follow up if we need more information about something you submitted. We do not sell, rent, or share your email address with third parties.

Cookies and advertising

Freebies Near Me does not currently run display advertising. If display advertising is added in the future, third party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time through Google's Ads Settings.

Content moderation

Listing submissions are automatically screened and reviewed by our team before appearing live on the site, to keep out spam and inappropriate content. See our About page for more on how that works.

Your choices

  • You can unsubscribe from giveaway alert emails at any time using the link in those emails.
  • You can ask us to delete a listing or update you submitted by reaching out through our feedback form.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site changes. Meaningful changes will be noted on this page with an updated date above.

Questions

Reach out any time through our feedback form.
